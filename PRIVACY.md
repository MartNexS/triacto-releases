# TRIACTO Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy describes TRIACTO on the web and Android (`com.triacto.app`). MartNexS is responsible
for TRIACTO. For privacy, access, correction, or deletion questions, contact
**martnexs.trrr@gmail.com**. Identify TRIACTO and explain the issue, but do not send passwords,
identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive information. TRIACTO has no user accounts.

## Data stored on the device

TRIACTO stores the selected language, music, sound and vibration preferences, chosen display name,
a random client identifier, and a legacy word-pack setting in browser or app storage. The legacy
setting no longer restricts content. This data remains until the player clears the site's/app's
data or uninstalls the Android app.

Offline pass-the-phone gameplay does not send match information to MartNexS.

## Optional online multiplayer

When a player creates or joins an online room, TRIACTO sends the information required to run and
synchronize that match to Convex: display name and random client identifier; room code and
language; team/player names and seating; scores, turn and role state, timers, votes, typed guesses,
and match progress; and connection, last-seen, creation, and activity timestamps.

The connection uses HTTPS and secure WebSocket transport. An hourly backend task deletes each room
after 24 hours without activity. MartNexS does not use room data for advertising, profiling, or
sale. Players should use nicknames and avoid putting personal information in names or guesses.

Convex provides the online database and real-time connection. Netlify and itch.io host the browser
editions. GitHub Releases and Google Play may distribute Android builds. These providers may
process ordinary technical data such as IP address, request time, browser/device headers, download
or store-account metadata, and security logs under their own policies. Processing may occur
outside the player's country according to their infrastructure and legal terms. Data is not shared
with advertisers or data brokers.

## Permissions and security

The Android app uses internet/network-state access for delivery and optional online play, audio
settings for music and effects, optional vibration, and wake-lock support while active. It does
not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, general file storage, overlay, or
foreground media-service access. Recording and background playback are disabled.

TRIACTO limits player-authored field lengths, removes emoji from those fields, validates online
actions on the server, and uses encrypted transport. No system can guarantee absolute security;
share online room codes only with intended participants.

## Audience, advertising, purchases, and web support

TRIACTO is intended for ages 13 and over. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied
personal information may contact MartNexS with the approximate time and room code, if available.

TRIACTO has no ads, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, behavioral tracking, in-app purchases,
subscriptions, paid packs, or Google Play Billing integration. All game content is included free.
The web edition's “Support TRIACTO” button opens Ko-fi for an optional tip processed by Ko-fi and
its payment providers. A tip does not unlock content, remove restrictions, confer status, or
provide any game advantage. TRIACTO does not receive payment-card data. The Google Play Android
app contains no donation wording or Ko-fi destination and instead links only to the public app
website.

Material policy changes will be identified by an updated date. The canonical policy is available
at https://martnexs.com/privacy/triacto/.
